About the Ministry

Sri Lanka Baptist Men's Fellowship

Since 1916

On 19th February 1916, the Baptist Women's League (BWL) was formed during a gathering of European missionaries and Ceylonese ladies from the Cinnamon Gardens Baptist Church, Kotikawatte, Hendala, Grandpass, Biyanwila, and Moratuwa Baptist Churches at the newly built Waldock Memorial Hall in the Carey College premises, Colombo.

The first ministry of the BWL was launched by women Bible teachers and evangelists. They went from house to house in the villages and in the slums in Colombo, reaching out to poor women — teaching them about cleanliness, good habits, good conduct, and literacy, while sharing the love of Jesus and praying with them.

From 1926, the BWL held Women's Conferences twice a year which included worship, Bible studies, discussions, handicraft workshops, and participation in singing and drama. These gatherings became a cornerstone of spiritual enrichment and community for women across the island.

The BWL has from time to time provided relief to victims of war, floods, the Tsunami, and other natural disasters. They have also conducted Eye and Medical Clinics in many areas. After the Tsunami in 2004, the BWL launched a long-term programme in Weligama and Telwatte Ampegama with the assistance of the Hong Kong Baptist Church, the International Women's Missionary Movement, and Baptist Aid Australia. The victims received self-employment training and children were given additional classes to assist them in their studies.

Presently there is a Women's Fellowship (BWL) in each and every church. Regular events and activities include Special Family Programmes, Fund-raising events, Special BWL Sunday, Residential Camp, Leadership Programmes, and Bible Olympics. The BWL has a rich history of service and continues to impact lives in Sri Lanka, serving the community and promoting spiritual growth among women.

Historical content extracted from "History of Baptist Churches and The Auxiliaries".

What We Do

Projects and Activities

Women's Conference

Annual and bi-annual gatherings featuring worship, Bible teaching, fellowship, discussions, and handicraft workshops drawing women from churches across the island.

Community Outreach

Visiting homes, supporting families in slums and rural areas, providing disaster relief, and conducting medical and eye clinics in communities across Sri Lanka.

Prayer Ministry

Regular prayer meetings interceding for churches, missionaries, the nation, and families. Prayer chains connect BWL members island-wide in unified intercession.

Disaster Relief

Providing support to victims of war, floods, tsunami, and natural disasters — including self-employment training and educational support programmes for children and families.

Leadership Programmes

Equipping women with spiritual and practical leadership skills through residential camps, Bible Olympics, and training sessions to serve faithfully in their churches.

Special BWL Sunday

An annual churchwide Sunday dedicated to celebrating the BWL, honouring women in ministry, and affirming their vital role in the life and mission of each local church.

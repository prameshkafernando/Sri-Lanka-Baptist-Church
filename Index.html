Who We Are

About Us

The Sri Lanka Baptist Church connects and serves Baptist churches throughout Sri Lanka, promoting fellowship, evangelism, discipleship, and community development since 1812.

With over 30 churches and numerous mission points spanning all districts, we are committed to the Great Commission — reaching every soul with the love of Christ.

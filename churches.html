Our Fellowship

Baptist Churches Across Sri Lanka

The Sri Lanka Baptist Church is home to a family of Baptist churches spread across every corner of the island. From the bustling city of Colombo to the lush highlands of Kandy, from the northern plains of Kilinochchi to the southern coast of Moratuwa — our churches share one faith, one fellowship, and one mission.

Each local church is an independent congregation united under the banner of the Church, worshipping together, serving their communities, and proclaiming the Good News of Jesus Christ to every person in Sri Lanka.

Whether you are looking for a place to worship, seeking fellowship, or want to know more about Baptist ministry near you — this directory is your starting point.

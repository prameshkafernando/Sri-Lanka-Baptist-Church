Our Fellowship

Baptist Churches Across Sri Lanka

The Sri Lanka Baptist Church is home to a family of Baptist churches spread across every corner of the island, organised into four zones — Colombo, Siyane, Kandurata and Kelaniweli. From the bustling city of Colombo to the northern plains of Chavakachcheri, from the hills of Kandy to the southern coast of Moratuwa — our churches share one faith, one fellowship, and one mission.

Each local church is an independent congregation united under the banner of the Sangamaya, worshipping together, serving their communities, and proclaiming the Good News of Jesus Christ to every person in Sri Lanka — together with the mission points and mission stations many of them oversee.

Whether you are looking for a place to worship, seeking fellowship, or want to know more about Baptist ministry near you — this directory is your starting point.
